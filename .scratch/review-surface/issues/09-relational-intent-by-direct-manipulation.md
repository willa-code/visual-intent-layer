# 09: Express Relational Intent by direct manipulation

**What to build:** With the Arrange tool, manipulating selected targets expresses that they should be ordered, aligned, equally spaced, contained, equivalent, or comparatively sized. The relation is shown back as one plain sentence, stored as a desired relationship rather than a pixel displacement, and delivered to the agent, which chooses the implementation. Experimenting stays reversible and never changes the artifact's source. The relation type and operator pickers are gone.

**Blocked by:** 08 (Region and multi-target selection)
